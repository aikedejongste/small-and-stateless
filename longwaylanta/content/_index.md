---
title: "Long Way Lanta"
---

Two friends, two motorbikes, one long way: from Maaspoort in Den Bosch to the
Kohub coworking space on Koh Lanta. Overland where possible, on Dutch plates,
timed to roll into the Andaman dry season.

This site is the living plan: the route, the day-to-day schedule, the bikes,
and — most importantly — the decisions we've made and why.
