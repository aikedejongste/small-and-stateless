House rules for better chats. Every rule comes with a right and a wrong example, shown the way they'd look in the chat itself. Green bubble: do this. White bubble: avoid this.
1

Write questions so they can be answered with yes

RightCan we meet at 10?14:02 ✓✓
WrongWhen would suit you?14:02 ✗
2

Write questions so that yes is your preferred answer

RightShall I book the restaurant for 19:00?14:05 ✓✓
WrongShall I not book yet?14:05 ✗
3

If you want something, be specific

RightCan you send me the invoice from March?14:08 ✓✓
WrongCan you send me that thing?14:08 ✗
4

If you reply to a message, quote it

Right
Friday works
Great, confirmed14:11 ✓✓
WrongYes that's fine(three topics later, nobody knows what)14:11 ✗
5

Just send your question, no separate 'hi'

RightHi! Can you bring the drill tomorrow?14:14 ✓✓
WrongHi...waits for reply... "Can you bring the drill?"14:14 ✗
6

Be explicit

RightCan we turn on the AC?14:17 ✓✓
WrongIt's warm here14:17 ✗
7

One topic per message

RightTwo messages: one about the meeting, one about the invoice14:20 ✓✓
WrongOne message mixing the meeting, the invoice, and weekend plans14:20 ✗
8

Put the deadline in the question itself

RightCan you confirm before Friday 17:00?14:23 ✓✓
WrongCan you confirm soon?14:23 ✗
9

Name the person, not "someone"

RightMarco, can you check the router?14:26 ✓✓
WrongSomeone should check the router14:26 ✗
10
