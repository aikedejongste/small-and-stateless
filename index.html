Small & Stateless

A handful of small sites and apps that each do one thing. No backend, no accounts, nothing saved — most of them are a single HTML file.

Logical Fallacies

/fallacies

A practice quiz for spotting flawed reasoning. Read a statement, name the fallacy, and find out why it breaks — twenty of the usual suspects, from ad hominem to the sunk cost fallacy.

quiz 20 fallacies reasoning

Hebrew Flashcards

/hebrew

Flip cards for a few hundred everyday Hebrew words, with transliteration and English. Sorted into essentials, verbs, numbers, time, social phrases and more, so you can drill one category at a time.

flashcards 310 words 8 categories

Lunar Sleep Analysis

/sleep

A year of nightly sleep scores plotted against the phase of the moon, to see whether the full moon really does anything. Sleep score, resting heart rate and duration, broken down by lunar phase.

data moon phases sleep tracking

WhatsApp Guidelines

/comm

Eighteen rules for writing messages people can actually answer. Ask things that can be answered with yes, put the deadline in the question, name the person instead of "someone", and skip the separate "hi".

writing 18 rules communication

Berlin Neighborhoods

/hoods

An interactive map of Berlin's 96 Ortsteile across 12 Bezirke. Tap any neighbourhood to see which district it belongs to, or hit the crosshair to locate the one you're standing in.

map berlin 96 ortsteile

A hand-kept list of two dozen places to eat around Maaspoort and along the Maas in 's-Hertogenbosch, on a map and filterable by village, cuisine and price. In Dutch.

Fast Charger Coverage

/charger-coverage

How many Dutch motorway fuel stations also have an EV fast charger. 162 of 239 did — 67.8% — counted from OpenStreetMap in October 2025. A snapshot, not a live figure.

data 239 stations openstreetmap

/longwaylanta

Two friends riding two motorbikes from Den Bosch to Koh Lanta — roughly 17,000 km across 18+ countries on Dutch plates. The route on a map, the day-to-day plan, the bikes, and a written record of every decision and why.
